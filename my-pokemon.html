My Pokémon

Import from Poke Genie

  1. In Poke Genie, open your scan history and export it as a CSV file.
  2. Tap a button below and pick that file.

Replaces your saved list with everything in the file.

For a small export of just the Pokémon you rescanned: those get updated, new ones get added, and everything else stays as it was.

Duplicate scans are merged automatically. A Pokémon you marked as gone comes back if you scan it again. Your file never leaves this device.

Ignore old scans

Pokémon you haven't scanned in a while may have been traded or transferred.

Marked as gone

Tap ✕ on any of your Pokémon in Boss Lookup to hide it here.

Backup & other devices

Save everything (your Pokémon, hidden list, settings) to a file, then load it on another phone or browser.
